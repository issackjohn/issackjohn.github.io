I Got ChatGPT to Monitor the Visa Bulletin for Me

If you're waiting for your green card priority date to become current, you've probably checked the Visa Bulletin more times than you'd like to admit.

Instead of manually checking the State Department page every month, I set up a scheduled ChatGPT prompt to monitor it for me.

The Prompt

Check the official U.S. Department of State Visa Bulletin page regularly.

Monitor EB-2 for India under both Final Action Dates and Dates for Filing against priority date January 15, 2023.

Only notify me if:
1. A new monthly bulletin is published.
2. The cutoff dates move or my priority date becomes current.

Include the previous date, new date, current status, and a link to the official bulletin.

Swap in your category, country of chargeability, and priority date.

Note: Use this as a notification trigger, not legal advice. When an alert fires, always verify it directly against the official Visa Bulletin and USCIS charts.

It took two minutes to set up, and it eliminates having to remember to check a government site every month.
